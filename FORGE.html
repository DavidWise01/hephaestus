THE FORGE

An artifact that builds better artifacts

Hephaestus does not just hold an artifact — he improves it. Feed the forge a weak artifact and it works the weakest article each pass, applying a named craft, emitting a new, strictly-better child — a hash-linked lineage that climbs until every article clears its threshold and the artifact is constitutional (the bar set by engine 4.1). Pure logic, no dependencies; it runs anywhere.

Forge State

Lineage · provenance chain

WHAT THIS IS: the heart of Hephaestus, made to run. Each pass takes the artifact's lowest-scoring article, applies its craft (diminishing-returns lift), and mints a new artifact whose parent is the previous one's hash — so the lineage is a real provenance chain, every child strictly better than its parent, ending in one that satisfies all six constitutional articles. The forge is itself an artifact (one file); given an artifact, it returns a better one. That recursion is the whole idea.
